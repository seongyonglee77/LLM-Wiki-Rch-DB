## Bibliographic Metadata

- Year: 2023
- Design: empirical

## One-sentence Summary

A qualitative study shows that ChatGPT can take several instructional roles, but teachers remain responsible for orchestration, learner agency, and ethical judgment.

## Keywords

ChatGPT; large language model; teacher-AI collaboration; language education; AI ethics

# Structured Summary

## Purpose

To examine how language teachers actually used ChatGPT and how teacher and chatbot roles complemented one another.

## Method

Eleven language teachers used ChatGPT for two weeks; interview data and interaction logs were analyzed qualitatively.

## Findings

ChatGPT functioned as interlocutor, content provider, teaching assistant, and evaluator. Teachers orchestrated resources and pedagogical decisions, positioned students as active investigators, and addressed ethical use of AI.

## Discussion & Conclusion

The paper frames ChatGPT as a complement rather than a replacement for teachers. Its usefulness depends on teacher expertise, verification, and purposeful task design.
