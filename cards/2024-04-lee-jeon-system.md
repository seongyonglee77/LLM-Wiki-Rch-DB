- Year: 2024
- Design: empirical

## One-sentence Summary

A multiple-case study shows that primary English teachers enacted assessment agency by selectively adapting ICT to local goals, institutional expectations, and post-pandemic classroom conditions.

## Keywords

teacher agency; classroom-based assessment; ICT affordances; ecological perspective; primary English education

# Structured Summary

## Purpose

To investigate how teacher agency and ICT affordances shaped classroom-based assessment after the return to face-to-face teaching.

## Method

Two primary school English teachers were examined through observations, interviews, and reflective data; reflexive thematic analysis was combined with a multiple-case comparison.

## Findings

Teachers used ICT differently: one expanded formative assessment and communication-oriented practices, while the other used an LMS more minimally within a test-oriented context. District expectations, autonomy, training, and professional development mediated these choices.

## Discussion & Conclusion

ICT affordances do not determine assessment practice. Agency is ecological and temporal, emerging from teachers’ prior experience, future goals, present constraints, and institutional culture.
