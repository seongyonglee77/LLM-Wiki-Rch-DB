## One-sentence Summary

Speech-recognition chatbot practice was perceived positively yet reinforced native-speaker pronunciation norms, revealing a tension between AI feedback and Global Englishes principles.

## Keywords

speech recognition; Global Englishes; AI bias; pronunciation; ELT

# Structured Summary

## Purpose

To examine learners’ attitudes toward English and pronunciation after using a speech-recognition chatbot through a Global Englishes lens.

## Method

Reflection papers from 38 students were analyzed with text mining/sentiment analysis and interviews with seven students were thematically analyzed.

## Findings

Learners valued private, repeatable practice and confidence-building feedback, but the chatbot’s recognition bias led them to treat Korean-accented English as wrong and native-like pronunciation as the target.

## Discussion & Conclusion

Accessibility and practice benefits can coexist with sociolinguistic harm. ELT integration therefore requires explicit critical AI literacy and exposure to diverse Englishes.
