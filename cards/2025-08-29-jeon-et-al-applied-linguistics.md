- Year: 2025

## One-sentence Summary

A translanguaging perspective reframes GenAI as a flexible but power-laden participant in meaning-making, requiring attention to multilingual practices, bias, and human agency.

## Keywords

generative AI; translanguaging; multilingualism; language ideology; critical AI literacy

# Structured Summary

## Purpose

To theorize GenAI use through translanguaging and expose tensions between flexible language practices and AI’s statistical, standardized tendencies.

## Method

Conceptual/theoretical analysis drawing on translanguaging, AI, language education, and sociolinguistic scholarship.

## Findings

GenAI can receive mixed-language input and support flexible communication, yet its training data, dominant-language patterns, and confident output can reproduce hierarchies and constrain users’ linguistic identities.

## Discussion & Conclusion

Critical pedagogy should treat AI output as material for negotiation rather than truth, making power, ownership, bias, and multilingual meaning-making visible.
