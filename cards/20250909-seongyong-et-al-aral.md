## One-sentence Summary

The study demonstrates that carefully refined ChatGPT prompts and Global Englishes-based rubrics can improve feedback relevance, but generic feedback still risks erasing learners’ individual linguistic and cultural identities.

## Keywords

Global Englishes; generative AI; ChatGPT; feedback; language assessment

# Structured Summary

## Purpose

To explore whether GenAI can provide English-learning feedback aligned with World Englishes/English as a Lingua Franca principles.

## Method

ChatGPT-generated assessment/feedback outputs were iteratively refined using Global Englishes scholarship and evaluated across tasks/models.

## Findings

The refined model produced more relevant guidance on communicative effectiveness, pragmatic strategies, cultural relevance, and creative language use, yet remained standardized and insufficiently personalized.

## Discussion & Conclusion

Prompt and rubric design can orient GenAI toward Global Englishes, but human review and learner-specific context remain necessary.
