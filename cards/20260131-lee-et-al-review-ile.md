## One-sentence Summary

A systematic review of 49 classroom-based empirical studies finds higher-education and English dominance, heavy reliance on self-report and writing research, diverse GenAI roles, and persistent quality, pedagogy, integrity, and overreliance concerns.

## Keywords

generative AI; language learning; classroom research; systematic review; learner experience

# Structured Summary

## Purpose

To synthesize learner-centered classroom research on GenAI in L2/foreign-language learning and identify research and implementation gaps.

## Method

Forty-nine empirical studies published from January 2023 to December 2024 were reviewed by setting, target language, methods, learner focus, GenAI roles, and challenges.

## Findings

GenAI most often served as feedback provider, tutor, cognitive stimulator, interaction facilitator, or conversation partner. Studies centered on learner attitudes, self-efficacy, motivation, and writing, while reporting querying/content-quality problems, pedagogical limits, overreliance, and academic-integrity concerns.

## Discussion & Conclusion

Future work should diversify skills, tools, contexts, and methods; include K–12, longitudinal and behavioral evidence; and develop customized applications for personalization.
