The chapter argues that VEO-integrated IMDAT supported evidence-based reflection and feedback through which one pre-service teacher diversified questioning practices, elicited more learner contributions, and developed classroom interactional competence.

Structured Summary

Purpose

To show how a focal pre-service English teacher changed questioning practices over time within a VEO-integrated IMDAT teacher education framework.

Findings

The study uses six discourse events over time to demonstrate change in Belma's questioning practices. At Time 1, Belma's yes/no interrogatives and negative-polarity questions received limited student responses.
