One-sentence Summary

The study shows how teachers’ professional learning and pedagogical judgments shape the educational value of AI chatbots in language teaching.

Keywords

AI chatbot; teacher professional development; language teaching; pedagogical judgment; agency

Structured Summary

Purpose

To understand teachers’ experiences, learning, and instructional decisions while integrating AI chatbots into language-teaching practice.

Method

The source reports a qualitative investigation organized around teacher experience, AI-supported instructional use, and reflective professional development; interview/reflection evidence was thematically interpreted.

Findings

Teachers saw chatbots as useful for generating and adapting materials, supporting interaction, and extending practice, while also encountering output quality, reliability, and pedagogical-alignment concerns.

Discussion & Conclusion

Professional development should move beyond tool operation toward critical evaluation, task orchestration, and alignment with learner needs and curricular aims.
