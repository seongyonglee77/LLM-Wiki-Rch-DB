One-sentence Summary

A 16-week quasi-experiment shows that both a presentation task and an AI-chatbot task in a 3D metaverse increased preservice teachers’ Global Englishes awareness, with the chatbot condition having stronger effects on ELF confidence and intention.

Keywords

Global Englishes; ELF; AI chatbot; 3D metaverse; preservice teachers; GELT

Structured Summary

Purpose

To test whether ELF interaction with AI chatbots in a 3D metaverse can raise preservice English teachers’ Global Englishes awareness and support a computer-assisted GELT approach.

Method

Ninety-seven South Korean preservice English teachers were assigned to a control group (n=32), presentation-task group (n=31), or AI-chatbot-in-3D-metaverse group (n=34) across a 16-week teacher-training course. Pre/post surveys, ANCOVA, and interviews were used.

Findings

Both technology-supported tasks positively affected acceptance of local and other Englishes, native-speakerism, ELF confidence/intention, and willingness to incorporate Global Englishes Language Teaching. The AI-chatbot task had a stronger effect on ELF confidence and intention.

Discussion & Conclusion

AI chatbots and immersive environments can make Global Englishes and ELF interaction experiential, but their value depends on task design that foregrounds diversity, negotiation, and teacher education rather than native-speaker norms.
