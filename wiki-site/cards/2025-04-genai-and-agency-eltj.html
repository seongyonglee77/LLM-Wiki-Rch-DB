One-sentence Summary

The study positions preservice teacher agency as the mechanism through which GenAI affordances are critically selected, adapted, and converted into pedagogically meaningful ELT work.

Keywords

generative AI; preservice teachers; teacher agency; ELT; teacher education

Structured Summary

Purpose

To examine how preservice teachers enact agency when using GenAI for English-language-teaching preparation.

Method

An empirical teacher-education study analyzed preservice teachers’ GenAI-supported design/reflection work and its implications for agency and ELT practice.

Findings

GenAI expanded ideation and material-production possibilities, but meaningful learning depended on evaluating outputs, revising them for context, and maintaining pedagogical ownership.

Discussion & Conclusion

Agency is distributed across people, tools, tasks, and institutional conditions; teacher education should cultivate critical, ethical, and context-sensitive AI use.
