One-sentence Summary

Design-based work with GenAI and a metaverse strengthened preservice English teachers’ applied-linguistics content knowledge when they iteratively calibrated AI outputs and reflected on reliability, bias, authorship, and inclusion.

Keywords

generative AI; metaverse; preservice teachers; content knowledge; learner agency

Structured Summary

Purpose

To examine how GenAI and metaverse affordances, learner agency, and reflection mediate content-knowledge development in teacher education.

Method

Twenty-three preservice English teachers designed personal brands and 3D showrooms. Reflective journals and semi-structured interviews were analyzed thematically.

Findings

Content knowledge developed as abstract concepts were enacted in audience-specific artefacts through planning, creation, evaluation, and revision. Participants moved toward learning with GenAI as a co-designer while exercising distributed agency and critical AI literacy.

Discussion & Conclusion

Technology-mediated design can connect disciplinary theory with practice when teacher education includes modelling, authentic tasks, collaboration, scaffolding, and reflection rather than technocentric tool training.
