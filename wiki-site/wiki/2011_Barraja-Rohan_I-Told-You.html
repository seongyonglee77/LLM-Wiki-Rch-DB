'I told you': Storytelling development of a Japanese learning English as a Second Language

Summary

The chapter shows that Akiko's storytelling developed from short, response-based tellings into longer, more complex, socially consequential narratives, while emphasizing that this development remained gradual, uneven, and interactionally demanding.

Key Findings

In the first interaction, Akiko mostly remains a listener and produces only a very short story. By the second interaction, Akiko produces more telling, longer turns, and more complex grammar than before. By the third interaction, Akiko sustains an intricate extended telling, but does so in a fragmented and interrupted way.
