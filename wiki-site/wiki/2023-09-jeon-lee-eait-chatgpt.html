Summary

A qualitative study shows that ChatGPT can take several instructional roles, but teachers remain responsible for orchestration, learner agency, and ethical judgment.

Key Findings

ChatGPT functioned as interlocutor, content provider, teaching assistant, and evaluator. Teachers orchestrated resources and pedagogical decisions, positioned students as active investigators, and addressed ethical use of AI.
