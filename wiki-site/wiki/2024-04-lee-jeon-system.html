Teacher agency and ICT affordances in classroom-based language assessment: The return to face-to-face classes after online teaching

  • Card: summary card
  • Source: parsed source
  • Bibliography key: `@leejeon2024agency`
  • Metadata status: `open`

Summary

A multiple-case study shows that primary English teachers enacted assessment agency by selectively adapting ICT to local goals, institutional expectations, and post-pandemic classroom conditions.

Key Findings

Teachers used ICT differently: one expanded formative assessment and communication-oriented practices, while the other used an LMS more minimally within a test-oriented context. District expectations, autonomy, training, and professional development mediated these choices.
