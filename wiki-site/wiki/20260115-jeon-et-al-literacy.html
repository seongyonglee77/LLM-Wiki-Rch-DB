Summary

The framework argues that AI literacy must move beyond tool use toward critical, situated, and inclusive meaning-making in which learners infuse human experience and validate outputs in real contexts.

Key Findings

AI’s fluent and anthropomorphic output can encourage uncritical acceptance, homogenization, and inequity. The proposed response emphasizes audience, production, critical evaluation, infusion of meaning, and action/community validation.
