AI and Language Education: A Comprehensive Map

AI research must look beyond the functionality of chatbots and GenAI themselves to examine what roles they play in language learning contexts, how teachers and learners exercise judgment, and what inequalities they reproduce.

Research Trajectories

1. Early chatbot research explored the possibilities of conversational partners, speaking and listening practice, and repetitive practice. 2. Comparative and experimental studies show that chatbots can match humans on some language outcomes but may lag in sustained interaction and social support. 3. GenAI research has expanded roles to feedback provider, tutor, cognitive stimulator, and interaction facilitator. 4. Recent work treats teacher agency, Global Englishes, critical AI literacy, and multimodality as core conditions.
