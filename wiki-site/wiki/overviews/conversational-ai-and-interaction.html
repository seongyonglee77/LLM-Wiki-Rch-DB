Conversational AI and Human Interaction

This overview page brings voice assistants, customer-service AI, conversational user interfaces, LLM agents, and language-education interaction into a single thread. The shared question is not only how accurately AI processes users' utterances, but also how it handles interruption, silence, repair, turn design, cultural positioning, and accessibility.

Key Perspectives

  • Dialogue is not an exchange of input and output but an interaction jointly constituted by turns, response expectations, repair, and social roles.
  • Rather than making users adapt their speech to system constraints, good systems should accommodate diverse speaking rates, interruptions, incompleteness, and cultural pragmatics.
  • Performance evaluation should include not only task accuracy but also interactional naturalness, explainability, accessibility, and privacy and safety constraints.
