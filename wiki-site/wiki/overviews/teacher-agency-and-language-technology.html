Language Technology and Teacher Agency

In language technology research, teacher agency does not mean that tool functions automatically determine teaching. Rather, it is the process by which teachers select and reconfigure technological affordances to fit their goals, context, and professional judgment. This page connects CALL, AI, and teacher education research.
