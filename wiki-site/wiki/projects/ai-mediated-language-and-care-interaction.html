AI-Mediated Language and Care Interaction

This project hub connects language education, teacher education, voice assistants, smart-home care, and customer-service automation as shared design and evaluation problems. The success criterion for implementation tasks is not whether the model generates sentences, but whether it provides sustainable help that aligns with real users' goals, vulnerabilities, and interaction patterns.

Project Axes

1. **Accessible Voice Assistants:** Treat interruptions, long pauses, and disfluencies not as user deficits but as variation the system must handle. 2. **Situated Dialogue Design:** Reflect user positioning, cultural pragmatics, service purpose, and care relationships in turn design. 3. **Language Education and Teacher Education:** Place questioning, storytelling, video reflection, and interactional competence at the center of AI integration outcomes. 4. **Behavior and Safety Evaluation:** As complexity increases, jointly evaluate the LLM agent's behavior gap, privacy, hallucination, and accountability.
