How Should AI Support Human Interaction?

This question runs through all ten papers in this set. Rather than first asking whether AI can replace humans, it asks under what conditions AI extends and under what conditions it interferes with human speaking, caring, learning, and professional judgment.

Specific Questions

  • How can a system repair a user's interruption, silence, or incomplete utterance without treating it as an error?
  • What social roles and hierarchies do AI's questions and responses position users into?
  • How are agency and accountability distributed across the model, the user, the interface, and the institution?
  • How should the tension between task success and naturalness be evaluated alongside accessibility, safety, and cultural fit?
  • In language education, teacher education, and care contexts, what kind of interactional resource should AI be rather than a replacement?

How to Use

This question page is an entry point for research questions and literature review. Do not pre-set conclusions; instead, narrow the question by following each card's Findings, Discussion, and directly cited evidence.
