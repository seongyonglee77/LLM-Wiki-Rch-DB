## Summary

The chapter argues that VEO-integrated IMDAT supported evidence-based reflection and feedback through which one pre-service teacher diversified questioning practices, elicited more learner contributions, and developed classroom interactional competence.

## Key Findings

The study uses six discourse events over time to demonstrate change in Belma's questioning practices. At Time 1, Belma's yes/no interrogatives and negative-polarity questions received limited student responses. At Time 2, Belma elicited more student responses by diversifying questions and extending student utterances.
