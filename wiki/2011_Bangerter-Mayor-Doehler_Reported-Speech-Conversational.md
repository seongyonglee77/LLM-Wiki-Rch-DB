# Reported Speech in Conversational Storytelling During Nursing Shift Handover Meetings

## Summary

The article argues that nurses use direct reported speech in handover stories to dramatize non-routine events, display professional reasoning, convey complaints indirectly, legitimize decisions, and sustain a community of practice.

## Key Findings

The two handover corpora contained substantial numbers of direct reported speech instances. Quoted sources varied by unit, with surgery nurses more often quoting themselves and patients, and rehabilitation nurses most often quoting patients. DRS is usually prepared by a buildup in which situations and actions give way to reported speech near the story climax.
