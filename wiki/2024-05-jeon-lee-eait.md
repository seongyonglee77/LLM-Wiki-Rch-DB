# Can learners benefit from chatbots instead of humans? A systematic review of human-chatbot comparison research in language education

- Card: [[../cards/2024-05-jeon-lee-eait|summary card]]
- Source: [[../sources/2024-05-jeon-lee-eait|parsed source]]
- Bibliography key: `@leejeon2024humanai`
- Metadata status: `open`

## Summary

Across 11 experiments, chatbots matched human interaction for some linguistic outcomes but were weaker in sustained interactivity, elaboration, and socially supportive communication.

## Key Findings

Chatbots produced comparable utterance sophistication, vocabulary/grammar levels, and some speaking/listening gains. Humans were generally stronger for sustained interaction, informative elaboration, and maintaining positive long-term engagement.
