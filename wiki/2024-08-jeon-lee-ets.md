# The impact of a chatbot-assisted flipped approach on EFL learner interaction

- Card: [[../cards/2024-08-jeon-lee-ets|summary card]]
- Source: [[../sources/2024-08-jeon-lee-ets|parsed source]]
- Bibliography key: `@jeonlee2024flipped`
- Metadata status: `open`

## Summary

A six-week chatbot-assisted flipped approach improved elementary EFL learners’ interactive speaking and perceptions of learner–learner and learner–instructor interaction compared with worksheet practice.

## Key Findings

The chatbot group outperformed the control group on interactive speaking and reported stronger interaction perceptions. Interviews explained this through repeated, low-pressure practice and continuity between out-of-class and in-class work.
