# How do AI and human users interact? Positioning of AI and human users in customer service

## Summary

The study shows that AI voice assistants and Korean human users co-construct interactional roles through standardized AI prompts, honorifics, keyword-based responses, silence, and varying degrees of human-like conversation.

## Key Findings

The AI always opened the interaction with the same first pair-part, regardless of user needs. The AI's opening combined a feminized voice and a recommended speech style for users. Keyword responses were the dominant user second pair-part. The authors found four main user response types, and the pattern held across successful and failed cases.
