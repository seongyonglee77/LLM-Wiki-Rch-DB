## Summary

The paper argues that agency in smart homecare is not located in a single human or machine actor but is produced through coordinated interaction among disabled service users, care workers, virtual assistants, and home technologies.

## Key Findings

Ted's Alexa use lets him perform his part of shared care work while Ann does other tasks. Hybrid agency is accomplished through the timing of Ted's command, Alexa's response, and Ann's embodied work. Ted's repeated request upgrades treat Alexa as potentially responsive to recipient design. Ann's apology treats her help or non-help as accountable within the distributed action.
