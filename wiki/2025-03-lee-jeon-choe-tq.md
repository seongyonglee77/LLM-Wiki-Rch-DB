# Enhancing Pre-Service Teachers' Global Englishes Awareness with Technology: A Focus on AI Chatbots in 3D Metaverse Environments

- Card: [[../cards/2025-03-lee-jeon-choe-tq|summary card]]
- Source: [[../sources/2025-03-lee-jeon-choe-tq|parsed source]]
- Bibliography key: `@leejeonchoe2025tesol`
- Metadata status: `open`

## Summary

A 16-week quasi-experiment shows that both a presentation task and an AI-chatbot task in a 3D metaverse increased preservice teachers’ Global Englishes awareness, with the chatbot condition having stronger effects on ELF confidence and intention.

## Key Findings

Both technology-supported tasks positively affected acceptance of local and other Englishes, native-speakerism, ELF confidence/intention, and willingness to incorporate Global Englishes Language Teaching. The AI-chatbot task had a stronger effect on ELF confidence and intention.
