# Generative AI and metaverse in developing pre-service teachers’ content knowledge

- Card: [[../cards/20260606-lee-jeon-llt|summary card]]

## Summary

Design-based work with GenAI and a metaverse strengthened preservice English teachers’ applied-linguistics content knowledge when they iteratively calibrated AI outputs and reflected on reliability, bias, authorship, and inclusion.

## Key Findings

Content knowledge developed as abstract concepts were enacted in audience-specific artefacts through planning, creation, evaluation, and revision. Participants moved toward learning with GenAI as a co-designer while exercising distributed agency and critical AI literacy.
