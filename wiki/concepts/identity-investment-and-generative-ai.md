# Identity, Investment, and Agency in the Age of Generative AI

Generative AI should be understood not merely as a neutral tool for language learning but as an interactional resource that reconstitutes learners’ identities, investment, and sociomaterial relations. The key issue is not whether AI replaces learners, but what kinds of linguistic and social subjects learners can become through participation.
