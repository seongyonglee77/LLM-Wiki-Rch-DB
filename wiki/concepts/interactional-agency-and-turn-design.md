# Interactional Agency and Turn Design

This concept page treats agency not as fixed to a single actor but as distributed across human, AI, interface, and institutional contexts in the process of organizing turns and responses. Systems that recover from interruptions, teachers who generate questions, professionals who use reported speech, and developers who design turns with SSML may appear to be different cases, but all address “who enables and constrains the next action.”

## Analytical Lenses

- **Turns and Repair:** Silence, interruptions, clarification requests, and backchannels are interactional resources, not errors.
- **Positioning and Roles:** Terms of address, honorifics, and question formats position AI and users as customers, service providers, teachers, learners, and so on.
- **Distributed Agency:** Outcomes are not determined solely by the model’s intentions but emerge from the combination of prompts, data, interface, user interpretation, and institutional purposes.
- **Assessment and Accountability:** Naturalness or task success alone is insufficient; we must also consider to whom failures are attributed and the conditions of accessibility and safety.
