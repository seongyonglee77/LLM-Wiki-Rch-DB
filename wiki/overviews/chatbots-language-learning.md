# Chatbots and Language Learning Research Trajectories

The core axes of chatbot research are expanding conversational opportunities, speaking and listening development, reducing affective burden, and role division with human teachers.

## Key Comparisons

- Speech-recognition chatbots: focused on speaking/listening and the conversational partner role.
- Human–chatbot comparison: some language outputs are similar, but persistence, interactivity, and social attitude favor humans.
- Flipped learning: repeated out-of-class conversation can be linked to in-class activities.
- Bias issues: speech recognition can entrench specific pronunciation norms as the standard.
